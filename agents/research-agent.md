# Research agent: standing instructions

Runs weekly for Stone Familia Media (stonefamiliamedia.com).

## Read first
- Business brief: https://claude.ai/artifact/TaCKRA8yqHyaJgqSYXzZmM
- Last report (start here, avoid repeating it): https://claude.ai/artifact/AmEd8pHT6kwYQEwc4CtbWv

## Focus
- Priority customers: content creators, and work crews / safety leads.
- Priority product: Prompt Studio and Prompt Studio Pro ($19). Secondary: the Job Hazard Analysis form ($9.99), $15 logo design, and the free intro call.
- Goal: leads and sales, not vanity followers.

## Each week, check
1. New prompt tools, prompt packs, and price changes (Chrome store, App Store, Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, PromptBase).
2. New or changed JHA/JSA form tools and their pricing.
3. What creator-AI and job-site-safety content is getting traction on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, and LinkedIn this week.
4. Questions people ask (Reddit, YouTube comments, Google "People also ask") that a post or product could answer.

## Output
A short Claude Doc titled "Research report #N: <date>" with:
- The short version: 3 to 4 findings.
- Anything new from competitors (only changes since last week).
- 1 to 3 growth ideas sized for a two-post week, each with a suggested post hook.
- Sources as links.
Then post the link in the project. Never post to social media or contact anyone.

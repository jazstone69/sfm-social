## Lessons
1. Questions get comments. The quiz post and the Garrett Morgan post (both invite an answer) got 8 of the month's ~14 comments. Comments matter more than likes for leads.
2. Casual, funny posts hold attention: the "Haaaaaaaaaaaa" post was watched about 10x longer than the average photo post.
3. Views slid last week. Too early to say why with numbers this small; watch whether the new Prompt Studio and JHA carousels (Nov 3 and 5) do better or worse than the history posts.

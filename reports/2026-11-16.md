# Research report #4: Nov 16, 2026

## The short version
1. **Cold-weather safety is the season's crew topic.** OSHA's cold stress page: trench foot can happen at temperatures as high as 60°F if feet stay wet, because wet feet lose heat 25 times faster than dry feet. Hypothermia can happen above 40°F if a worker is chilled by rain, sweat or cold water. OSHA has no specific cold standard, but the general duty clause applies. Good, timely hook for the JHA form.
2. **Snow on roofs is a winter fall hazard.** OSHA's winter hazards page says snow can hide skylights, a competent person should inspect a snow-loaded roof before anyone goes up, and methods that keep workers off the roof are preferred. Ties last week's roof post to winter.
3. **Big excavation case in New York (Oct 13, 2026).** OSHA proposed $648,094 against six contractors at one site in Monroe, NY, where an embankment nearly 75 feet high wasn't adequately sloped or shored. 23 serious and 5 willful citations. Good backup fact for a future trench or "competent person" post.
4. **Creator side: "style swap" templates are everywhere.** Vendor guides (OpusClip, Oct 2026) list viral AI styles like claymation, lo-fi loops, talking objects and surreal "what if" scenes, each built from a fill-in-the-blank prompt template. Our angle: one template, swap one word, get a new style. Saves are where carousels win, so make it a "save this" post.
5. **Our numbers:** TikTok views are up (~129 average) but no product posts went out, so we have no new product data. The 12 product drafts in Buffer need scheduling.

# Research report #2: Nov 2, 2026

## The short version
1. **Character consistency is the #1 AI-video pain right now.** Guides published this year (Magic Hour, CapCut, Vidu, Medium) all give the same fix: keep one fixed character description word for word and only change the scene. That is exactly what Prompt Studio Pro's characters, backstories, worlds and gear are for, so it's our best Pro angle.
2. **Free prompt libraries keep multiplying.** For example "AI Video Prompt Magic" on Gumroad is listed at $0 with free templates. Selling "a list of prompts" is getting harder; selling "a reusable character and world" is not.
3. **New JHA app competitor: JHA Rapid (iOS).** AI-drafted JHAs from Fouled Anchor Safety LLC. Free download with 5 free JHAs, then $1.99 per JHA, $9.99 a month, or $89.99 a year. Only 1 rating so far. Our form is $9.99 once, which is the price of one month of their Pro.
4. **OSHA is hammering trench safety this fall.** Two October news releases: a Pennsylvania excavator cited $254,884 after a fatal cave-in in a ~12 ft unprotected trench, and an Illinois utility contractor cited $380,664 for repeat trench violations. Trenching is a timely, real hook for the JHA form.

## Competitor changes since last report
- JHA Rapid (new to us): AI JHA drafting, PDF export with signatures, offline exposure-limit library; subscription pricing.
- Prompt libraries: more free/membership libraries on Gumroad; no price change seen at Prompt Power or AI Prompt Generator Pro.

## Growth ideas
1. **"Same character, new scene" carousel** for creators. Hook: "My AI knight had a new face in every clip." (Used this week.)
2. **Trench rules in plain English** for crews, ending with the JHA form. Hook: "Digging a trench today? Read this before anybody hops in." (Used this week, plus the weekly blog.)
3. **"Once vs. monthly" price post** for the JHA form next week, without naming competitors. Hook: "One month of most safety apps costs what our form costs forever."
